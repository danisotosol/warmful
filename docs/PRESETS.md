# WARMFUL factory presets

Generated by `warmful_render presets-md > docs/PRESETS.md` from `source/dsp/FactoryPresets.cpp`.

All presets assume **MEDIUM** calibration. Values are pot positions in % and as a
clock face (7:00 = fully left, 12:00 = centre, 5:00 = fully right), so they can also be dialled on a
physical pedal; the plug-in is a behavioural model, so expect to fine-tune by ear there.
Secondary controls are set in the SECONDARY menu (A+B); routing in FX SETUP (A+D).
The packs are inspired by the artists' documented rigs and records, not endorsed by them;
the research behind them is in [`research/`](research/README.md).

- **MK.GEE** (MKG): 424 Edge, Portastudio Crush, Baritone Drop, Distorted Room, Dream Wobble, Bowed Pad, Mood Smear, Rockman Clean, Two Star Lead, Quadraverb Grain
