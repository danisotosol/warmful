## 2. Verified facts
- **Writing guitar.** He writes "mostly" on a **Yamaha parlor acoustic with a passive pickup**. He usually DIs it into an interface or a **Tascam 244 four-track** ("most of my demos"), or records on a **Zoom H4N**, an **SM58 into a DI**, or phone voice memos. He does not mic his guitar with condensers (Reverb).
- **Attitude to fidelity.** "Bleed is really cool and accidents are some of the best moments." Clarity as a standard of quality is a "constructed myth" (Reverb).
- **souvenir (2020).** Made "mostly inside the box… in Ableton, effecting things, editing things, comping" (Reverb) and "recorded mainly alone" (RANGE). Producers were Monsune, Leland Whitty, Alex Sowinski and Jacques Greene (Bandcamp). Some tracks have distortion and fuzz (Atwood, Hypebeast).
- **portrait of a dog (2023).** Tracked live off the floor with BADBADNOTGOOD at Marquee Sound (engineer Braden Sauder), through a **Midas TR04 console** into an **Ampex 1" tape machine**. They used one 1960s Canadian compressor ("I think a Pultec copy"), and "other than that we didn't really compress anything". A **car-sized plate reverb in the hallway** is "in every song". The room was small, with everyone within about 3 ft, so "the sound is defined by all the bleed". There was almost no editing (Reverb). It was mixed by Chris Koltay (Hi Bias, Detroit) and Nick Hobden (Greenhouse, Vancouver) (Bandcamp).
- **Who plays guitar on POAD.** Yano plays **acoustic guitar**. **Leland Whitty** plays **electric guitar** on most tracks, plus tenor sax, violin and viola. Jasper Marsalis plays acoustic on "in sun, out of sun". "So sweet" started life as a nylon-string demo (Bandcamp, Reverb).
- **Heavy Loop (2024).** Recorded with his live band at Port William Sound. Songs were jammed until they worked, and every take was different. The 30-minute title track "leans into noise music and free sound" (Juno, Metal, Innovative Leisure). Ben Maclean plays electric guitar live (Clunk).
- **Later work.** "Homerun 2021" uses Mk.gee's guitar tones. *Spin* has a 4-track acoustic song, and "The Door" pairs acoustic guitar with pedal steel (Bandcamp, Alper).

## 3. Sonic signature (measurable; **[inference]** unless it restates a fact above)
- **Gain.** Mostly clean. On POAD, the console and tape add a little 2nd-harmonic warmth, roughly 3–8 dB of soft drive. Fuzz shows up only as a colour on souvenir-era tracks.
- **EQ.** Warm and slightly dark top: a cassette-style roll-off of about 10–12 kHz on demos. The piezo/passive DI sounds mid-forward, and a little body comes back through compression.
- **Compression.** Light. POAD was "barely compressed" (fact). Demos get gentle squash from the cassette deck.
- **Modulation.** No chorus or phaser is documented. There is tape wow from the 244: about 0.4–0.8 Hz at ±3–8 cents, randomised.
- **Pitch.** Standard tuning; no baritone or octave work is documented.
- **Delay.** Rare. On souvenir-era tracks, use short tape slaps of about 150–200 ms with 1–2 repeats.
- **Reverb.** The plate is the signature: about 1.2–2 s decay, mixed at roughly 20–30 %. Home demos sound like a small boxy room under 0.6 s.
- **Gating.** None mechanical. His dynamics are arrangement-based: the band drops out and leaves guitar and voice (Clunk).
- **Lo-fi.** Cassette hiss, head bump, slight wow, and phone-memo band-limiting.
- **Stereo.** Mostly mono-ish centred acoustic. Width comes from the room and the plate.
- **Playing.** Fingerpicked or soft-strummed parlor or nylon with jazz-folk extended chords. Electric lead lines are clean and melodic (Whitty, Maclean).

## 4. Reference songs
1. **"song about the family house"** (POAD): solo vocal and acoustic (credits). A dry, close parlor sound with plate air.
2. **"portrait of a dog" / "always"** (POAD): Whitty's clean electric over the live band, tape, bleed and the hallway plate.
3. **"delicate"** (souvenir): Monsune production with a "fuzzy yet gentle" backdrop. The arrangement drops out to leave his guitar live.
4. **"strawberry!" / "what i can do to help"** (souvenir): distortion and in-the-box processing.
5. **"The Heavy Loop"** (2024): a 30-minute improvisation built from noise, loops and free sound.
6. **"It Comes And Goes" / "The Door"** (*Spin*): a 4-track acoustic song, and acoustic with pedal steel.
