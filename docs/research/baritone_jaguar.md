## 3. Sonic signature (measurable)
- **Pitch:**
  - Bb standard: low string Bb1 = 58.3 Hz, −6 st from E. C standard: 65.4 Hz, −4 st. B: 61.7 Hz, −5 st. A: 55 Hz, −7 st. Bass VI: E1 = 41.2 Hz, −12 st.
  - **[inference]** With the .065 tuned to Bb on 24", tension is roughly 0.9× a standard low E. The result is slightly slack, with a "rubber-band" feel ([Internet Tattoo](https://www.internettattoo.com/blog/mk-gee-guitar-sound-and-tone)) and a sharp pitch-glide on hard picks.
- **Real baritone vs pitch-shift:**
  - A shifter moves the whole spectrum down, including pickup and body resonances. That is why the mids sound "less like a guitar" and the high end is lost ([sevenstring](https://www.sevenstring.org/threads/tuned-down-vs-pitched-down.351906/)).
  - A real baritone keeps those resonances fixed at roughly 2–5 kHz **[inference]** while the fundamentals drop.
  - Shifting also adds latency (25–40 ms in WARMFUL) and chord artefacts; players trust only 2–4 semitones.
  - Fix in WARMFUL: put CHAR after PITCH with TILT pushed right (0.55–0.62) to restore presence.
- **Flatwounds:**
  - **[inference]** Upper harmonics are reduced, highs decay quickly, there is no string squeak, and the attack is a soft "thump". Model it as a low-pass around 2.5–3.5 kHz plus mild compression and a low-end lift. SWEETEN gives +2 dB at 120 Hz and opto compression.
- **Strangle switch:**
  - **[inference]** 0.003 µF into a ~330–500 kΩ load gives a first-order high-pass at about 105–160 Hz.
  - In WARMFUL, FILTER HighPass at AMT ≈ 0.37–0.40 gives 130–150 Hz, assuming f = 20·150^x.
- **Rhythm circuit:**
  - **[inference]** Neck pickup loaded by the 50k/0.01 µF network gives a low-pass around 2 kHz.
  - In WARMFUL, Tilt-style FILTER at 0.25 gives ~2.2 kHz, assuming f = 250·80^(2x).
  - LowPass style at 0.6 gives ~2.9 kHz, assuming f = 150·133^x.
- **Gain:**
  - 424 preamp pushed to the edge of breakup: DRIVE 0.3–0.45 (+11–16 dB).
- **Space:**
  - Short and boxy (room mics, plate). SPACE TIME 0.05–0.15.

## 5. WARMFUL recipes
(The existing "MKG Baritone Drop" is −5 st, SWEETEN, CASSETTE. These recipes avoid duplicating it.)

```

description: "24in Jaguar tuned to Bb on flatwounds into a pushed 4-track. Thumb or soft pick, let chords ring."

moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [MOVE, CHAR, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.58, 0.35, 0.25, 1.00, 0.10, 0.18, 1.00, 0.60]
secondary: [0.60, 0.45, 0.05, 0.50, 0.05, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: LowPass
why: MOVE PITCH −6 st 100 % wet = Bb standard, the most-cited Mk.gee tuning (offset forum, Galli copy); low DRIFT keeps it clean.
why: CHAR DRIVE after the shift: +12 dB edge-of-breakup = Tascam 424 used as a cranked preamp (Wikipedia, GW); TILT 0.58 restores the presence pitch-shifting removes (sevenstring).
why: DIFF SPACE short chamber/room = boxy, room-mic intimacy rather than big halls (Internet Tattoo, Yano on Absolutely).
why: TEX FILTER LP ≈2.9 kHz = flatwound top-end loss ("super warm dark", Reverb listing) and hides granular artefacts.
```
