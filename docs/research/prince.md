## 2. Verified facts
**Guitars**
- His main guitar from Dirty Mind (1980) onward was a Hohner HG-490 "Mad Cat" built by Morris in Japan. It has a Tele-shaped body with Strat-style single coils and a hardtail Strat bridge. Susan Rogers says he preferred it in the studio. The pickups were later swapped for Fender Vintage Noiseless, then Kinman (GC-MC).
- The bridge pickup sits closer to the bridge than on a Tele or Strat, which gives "more aggressive treble bite". For "Let's Go Crazy" it was on the bridge pickup with volume and tone both at 10, strung with GHS Boomers .011–.050 in standard tuning (GW1).
- The Cloud guitar is maple, neck-through, 24.75" scale, with an EMG SA in the neck and an EMG 81 in the bridge (GCOM). The album "Purple Rain" was played on the Hohner, not the Cloud (SOS-KISS).

**Amps**
- Mesa/Boogie Mark IIB, run as a clean platform:
  - Settings: Vol1 7 with Pull Bright, Treble 7, Bass 4, Mid 5, Master 8, Presence 6, full power.
  - Cabs: two Bag End 4x12s loaded with JBL D120F "to maximize headroom" (GW1).
- For the 1984 tour the heads were upgraded to Mark IIC+, with 180 W of clean headroom once the cabs were rewired to 8 Ω (GP1).
- Prince "mainly got his lead tones from pedals", and Bendinelli recalls he did not use Lead mode (GP1).
- Guitarcloud notes he also used a channel switcher and, later, separate clean and dirty amps (GC Mesa page).

**Pedals**
- He moved to Boss pedals in the 1999 era: SD-1, BF-2, DM-2, CE-2 and OC-2, with the DS-1 and HM-2 used in the studio (GC-FX).
- The CE-2 gave way to the VB-2 around 1984. Delays went DM-2 → DSD-2 → DD-3 → DD-6.
- From 1992 he moved to the MT-2, and later to the DS-2 plus BD-2 with no amp distortion at all.
- Wahs: Vox V846, Cry Baby, Colorsound (GC-FX).
- "Let's Go Crazy" chain (GW1):
  - DS-1: Dist 9, Tone 6, Level 7.
  - OC-2: Oct1 6.5, Oct2 10, Direct 7, on the riff and first solo.
  - Colorsound wah on the second solo.
  - BF-2: Manual 9, Depth 9, Rate 3, Res 10, kicked on "at a few key moments".
- Prince kept the pedalboard so he could retune sounds on the fly. "The settings changed constantly… during the recording of a song sometimes." He threw out a preset rack rig (RBMA).

**Recording**
- Jesse Johnson to Rogers: "His rhythm guitar is always dry, through direct box… on the left" (IZO).
- Guitars went to tape both miked (SM57 or MD421) and DI'd through a Countryman 85 (GC-ERA, MIX-LGC).
- "Let's Go Crazy" was cut live in a 100×50 ft warehouse with no isolation (MIX-LGC).
- Studio favourites (MIX-LGC, MIX-PR):
  - Lexicon Prime Time and Delta T delays.
  - AMS nonlinear reverb.
  - EMT 250 and EMT 140 reverbs.
  - The Sunset Sound live chamber.
- Leonard gated the bleed on the "Purple Rain" overheads and the live snare (MIX-PR).
- The kick went through an AMS RMX16 on "Reverse 2" so it stood in for a bassline. "No reverb on anything else" on "Kiss" (SOS-KISS).

**"Kiss"**
- David Z ran the hi-hat through a ~150 ms delay, played guitar chords through a Kepex gate, and used the hi-hat tracks to trigger the gate (MIX-KISS).
- Prince then added a James Brown-style lick (SOS-KISS). That guitar is "dry and gated" (SOS-KISS) with "a small amount of tape delay" (MIX-KISS).

**Other uses**
- Prince also ran Linn LM-1 outputs through his Boss board: HM-2, flanger, chorus and delay (RBMA, IZO).
- The BF-2 is on the guitars of "Paisley Park" and "She's Always in My Hair", and on the LM-1 snare of "If I Was Your Girlfriend" (GC-BF2).
- The DM-2 is on the LM-1 in "When Doves Cry" (GC-DM2).
- The "When Doves Cry" intro is OC-2 plus heavy distortion according to a secondary source (MUSICRADAR). Its engineer was Peggy McCreary, not Rogers.

## 3. Sonic signature (measurable)
**Gain**
- Clean rhythm: a DI or very-high-headroom amp with no breakup, bright and thin, played as 16th-note chicken-scratch on the high strings.
- Lead: DS-1 with dist at 9/10, a hard-clipping op-amp distortion, into a clean amp. Mids sit forward and the bridge single coil adds treble bite.

**EQ**
- Treble and presence up, bass down (Mesa Bass 4, Treble 7, Pull Bright). The JBL D120F speakers emphasise mids and highs (GW1).
- Rhythm parts on the "no-bass" records ("When Doves Cry", "Kiss") are rolled off below ~150 Hz **[inference]**.

**Compression**
- In the studio the DI rhythm is compressed and tight. The live truck had 1176, LA-3A and dbx 160 units, mostly on drums and vocals (MIX-PR).

**Modulation (pedal specs)**
| Pedal | Range | Prince setting |
|---|---|---|
| CE-2 chorus | ~4–5 ms BBD delay, triangle LFO, minimum ≈0.3 Hz | — |
| BF-2 flanger | 1–13 ms delay, LFO period 100 ms–16 s (≈0.06–10 Hz) | "Let's Go Crazy" Rate 3/10 ≈ 3.5 s period ≈ **0.3 Hz**, Res max: slow, resonant jet **[inference: log taper]** |
| VB-2 vibrato | 2–15 Hz | Guitarcloud suggests Rate at 1:00 ≈ ~6 Hz, Depth at 11:00, i.e. shallow **[inference]** |

**Pitch**
- OC-2 sub-octave at roughly 2/3 the level of the dry signal (Oct1 6.5 vs Direct 7), plus a −2-octave voice (Oct2 10). Fed into distortion it gives a buzzy, fuzz-like octave.
- Tape varispeed was used on vocals and bass (RBMA).

**Delay**
- DM-2 range is 20–300 ms, dark BBD. Prime Time and Delta T were the studio digital delays.
- "Kiss": a 150 ms rhythmic hi-hat delay plus a short tape echo on the guitar.

**Reverb**
- Rhythm guitar: dry.
- Ballads: EMT 250 plate, the live chamber, plus room ambience from the club mics ("Purple Rain").
- Gated (nonlinear) AMS reverb goes on drums, not guitar.

**Gating**
- "Kiss" chops guitar chords with a Kepex gate keyed by the hi-hat. At ≈111 BPM that is 16ths ≈ 7.4 Hz **[inference: tempo]**.

**Stereo**
- Rhythm guitar is hard left, mono and dry (IZO). Width comes from the room and reverb, not from the guitar.

**Dynamics**
- Prince held long high notes rather than "100 notes" (GP2).

## 4. Reference songs
1. **"Let's Go Crazy" (1984)**:
   - Riff and first solo: DS-1 + OC-2.
   - Climax solo: Colorsound wah + BF-2 bursts.
   - Recorded live in the warehouse, miked + DI.
2. **"When Doves Cry" (1984)**:
   - Intro: high-neck octave fuzz lead.
   - Track: no bass; the LM-1 goes through a DM-2.
   - Long guitar/synth solo at the end.
3. **"Purple Rain" (1983/84)**:
   - Hohner chords recorded live at First Avenue through the IIC+.
   - Long sustained solo.
   - Mix: EMT 250, Delta T and the chamber, plus heavy room ambience.
4. **"Kiss" (1986)**: dry DI funk lick, gated, with a hint of tape echo, no bass and no reverb.
5. **"If I Was Your Girlfriend" / "Paisley Park" / "She's Always in My Hair"**: slow BF-2 flange as an arrangement colour.

## 5. WARMFUL recipes

```
name: "PRN Crazy Flange Solo"
description: "DS-1-style lead into a clean Boogie with a slow, resonant jet sweep. Bridge pickup, bend and hold."
effects: [DRIVE, PHASER, SPACE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.64, 0.80, 0.23, 0.85, 0.22, 0.15, 1.0, 0.30]
secondary: [0.55, 0.42, 0.00, 0.50, 0.05, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- CHAR DRIVE: +29 dB stands in for the DS-1 at Dist 9 into a clean Mark IIB; TILT 0.64 for Pull Bright, Treble 7 and the MadCat bridge bite (GW1).
- MOVE PHASER: there is no flanger. 12 deep stages at 0.30 Hz (RATE 0.23) approximate the BF-2 at Rate 3, Depth 9, Res 10 (GW1, BF-2 spec).
- DIFF SPACE: small room, 15 % wet, for the un-isolated warehouse take (MIX-LGC).
- TEX SQUASH: light compression for the punch and sustain of a 180 W Boogie into JBLs (GP1).
```

```
name: "PRN Doves Octave Fuzz"
description: "Octave-down blend into brash fuzz plus a dark slap. Play the intro lick high on the neck."
effects: [FUZZ, PITCH, CASCADE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [MOVE, CHAR, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.60, 0.65, 0.00, 0.50, 0.54, 0.20, 1.0, 0.25]
secondary: [0.55, 0.40, 0.00, 0.55, 0.15, 0.35, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- MOVE PITCH: −12 st at a 50 % blend mimics the OC-2 (Oct1 6.5 vs Direct 7). It sits before the fuzz so the octave intermodulates like an OC-2 into distortion (GW1, MUSICRADAR).
- CHAR FUZZ: heavy distortion on the intro (MUSICRADAR). TILT stays below 0.7, so there is no blown-speaker breakup and no sputter.
- DIFF CASCADE: ~180 ms dark BBD at low feedback; the DM-2 was used on "When Doves Cry" (GC-DM2), on the Linn **[inference for guitar]**.
- TEX SQUASH: sustain for the held, raked notes.
```

```
name: "PRN Kiss Gate"
description: "Dry DI funk scratch chopped in 16ths (~111 BPM) with a ghost of tape echo. Play muted 9th chords high."
effects: [SWEETEN, TREMOLO, REELS, FILTER]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.60, 0.30, 0.90, 0.75, 0.416, 0.12, 1.0, 0.40]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.00, 0.50, 0.10, 0.30, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: HighPass
why:
- CHAR SWEETEN: a light preamp plus opto compression is Prince's "always dry, through direct box" rhythm sound (IZO).
- MOVE TREMOLO: square chop at ≈7.4 Hz (RATE 0.90) imitates the Kepex gate keyed by the hi-hat (MIX-KISS, SOS-KISS). Tap-sync it to the song.
- DIFF REELS: a single ~150 ms tape repeat at low level, for the "small amount of tape delay" (MIX-KISS); 150 ms echoes Z's hi-hat delay.
- TEX FILTER: HPF ≈150 Hz for the no-bass, no-reverb starkness of "Kiss" (SOS-KISS).
```

```
name: "PRN Purple Rain Chords"
description: "Barely-edged Boogie clean with a tight stereo shimmer and a big plate/hall. Let the Bb chords ring."
effects: [DRIVE, DOUBLER, SPACE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, TEXT, DIFF]
primary:   [0.55, 0.22, 0.00, 0.45, 0.50, 0.30, 1.0, 0.30]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.25, 0.50, 0.20, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- CHAR DRIVE: low drive for the IIC+ footswitch quirk ("lead mode on about halfway, so there's still no clean sound", GP1).
- MOVE DOUBLER: a 12 ms take with ~±22 c drift stands in for the CE-2 (~5 ms, triangle LFO) or VB-2 on the 1984 board (GC-ERA). There is no true chorus **[inference]**.
- TEX SQUASH: the live-truck LA-3A/1176 feel (MIX-PR).
- DIFF SPACE: 2.5 s hall at 30 % for the EMT 250, chamber and First Avenue ambience. That mix is documented on the vocal, so applying it to guitar is **[inference]** (MIX-PR).
```

```
name: "PRN Rain Solo"
description: "Singing Boogie lead with a 300 ms analog echo. Hold one high note and let it bloom."
effects: [DRIVE, DOUBLER, CASCADE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, TEXT, MOVE, DIFF]
primary:   [0.50, 0.82, 0.00, 0.25, 0.72, 0.35, 1.0, 0.45]
secondary: [0.60, 0.40, 0.15, 0.50, 0.20, 0.45, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- CHAR DRIVE: an SD-1 boosting a hot IIC+ (GC Mesa page, GP1).
- TEX SQUASH: right after the drive, so held notes sustain (GP2: "hold a high note").
- MOVE DOUBLER: subtle width, like the two Bag End cabs **[inference]**.
- DIFF CASCADE: 298 ms is the DM-2 maximum (20–300 ms, dark BBD), at moderate feedback (GC-DM2, DM-2 spec).
```

**Approximating missing pieces**
- **Flanger:** use PHASER with high AMOUNT. DOUBLER's minimum is 12 ms, too long to comb.
- **Wah:** there is no pedal wah. HOWL with TILT left gives an auto-wah alternative (the TW-1 era).
- **Gate:** use TREMOLO in square mode, or FUZZ with TILT right for a sputter gate.
- **Gated reverb:** short SPACE then SQUASH 0.8. Use it on drums only; it is not Prince's guitar sound.

## 6. Confidence and open questions
- **High:** MadCat, Mesa IIB/IIC+ clean platform, Boss board with the "Let's Go Crazy" settings, "Kiss" gate and tape delay, dry DI rhythm. All come from engineer or tech interviews or Guitar World.
- **Medium:** BF-2 Hz and VB-2 Hz conversions (pot taper assumed); OC-2 on the "When Doves Cry" intro (secondary analysis plus forums); "Kiss" tempo of ~111 BPM.
- **Open:**
  - Exact chorus/VB-2 settings on "Purple Rain".
  - Whether the "When Doves Cry" intro also used varispeed. This is a forum claim only, and no McCreary interview was found.
  - Whether the Eventide H949/H910 listed for Dirty Mind, Controversy and Purple Rain (GC list) touched guitar.
  - The Sign o' the Times-era chain, which had a Dytronic CS-5 and SPX-90 in the live rack (GC-ERA). These could justify a VIBRATO-based "PRN Paisley Vibe" preset (VB-2 ~6 Hz, ±7–12 c).
