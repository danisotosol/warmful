# Junior Mesa: sound research brief (MSA pack)

**Who this is.** Junior Mesa is Reynaldo Peter Mesa Jr. (AllMusic lists him as "Peter Reynaldo Mesa Jr"), born 9 July 1999 in Bakersfield, CA. He is a self-taught singer and multi-instrumentalist (guitar, bass, drums, keys), signed to Ricky Reed's Nice Life Recording Co. from 2019 to 2023 and self-releasing through Virgin Music Group distribution since 2025. Releases:
- *Peace* EP (2019)
- "Losing My Grip"/"Creep" (2020)
- *Cirque Du Freak* EP (2021)
- "Bleacher Blues"/"Buta Fly" (2022)
- *Quit Your Job, Runaway!* EP (2023)
- *Audiotree Live* (2023)
- singles "good time" and "im not your man" (2025), "do your best nicholas", "the cops are coming" and "id treat u better" (2026)
- debut album *eye/spy/skinwalker*, announced for 4 Dec 2026

**How I disambiguated.** Searches on "Junior Mesa" mostly return Mesa/Boogie amps, Fender's "Junior Collection" guitars and the baseball player José Mesa Jr. I kept only sources that tie the name to Bakersfield, Nice Life, his credits (legal name above, publisher "Nicotine Corn Dog Music") or the Instagram handle @juniorrrjunior. He was earlier in a rap group called Yellowbelly. A Threads post claims he made unreleased music with Kevin Abstract; I could not verify it, so nothing here relies on it.

## 2. Verified facts (gear, chain, recording)
- **He records most parts himself, at home or in small studios.**
  - *Peace*: recorded at "Junior's House"/"Mom's House" and Elysian Park; engineered by Mesa and Ethan Shumaker. Sean Lee adds guitars and **electric sitar** on "Ordinary". (Bandcamp *Peace*)
  - *Cirque*: four of five songs recorded at "Nicotine Corndog Studios" and engineered by Mesa. Mixed by Collin Dupuis (Center For Pleasure Mechanics), mastered by Daddy Kev. On "Pushing Away", Zack Sekoff co-produced and played guitar. (Bandcamp *Cirque*)
- **Who mixed, by release.**
  - 2023: Ricky Reed or Aidan Spiro; Nate Mercereau on additional production; strings by Michael J. Fleming on "Caroline". The **"You Are Alright"** writing credits include Stereolab's Laetitia Sadier and Tim Gane. (Bandcamp *QYJR*)
  - 2026: "the cops are coming" credits Mesa with electric guitar, synth, piano, drums, mixing and mastering, plus Sean Lee on electric guitar and Josh Shpak on synth. (YouTube credits; AllMusic)
- **Tools:** Ableton 11 (Luna 2021) and phone voice memos (Luna 2022). He learned on his grandfather's guitar; the model is not documented. (BMI)
- **Live band:** Mesa on guitar and vocals, Sean Lee on lead guitar, Julian Cullars on bass, Alex Smith on drums, Hunter Meelee on keys. (Audiotree)
- **No source names his guitar models, amps, pedals or tape machines.** No Equipboard page or rig rundown exists. Every Tascam 424 result I found refers to Mk.gee, not him.

## 3. Sonic signature (measurable; [inference] unless cited)
- **Gain:** warm preamp level through to "saturated and distorted" (cited, cops). I estimate +12 to +20 dB, soft clipping and mostly even harmonics, never metal-tight. Lead outros go into round, fuzzy territory (Hendrix lineage; "Beatlesque" outro, cited).
- **EQ:** mids-forward and dark. Treble is rolled off around 5–8 kHz like cassette playback (the "old cassette… rattling speakers" line, cited), with a head bump around 80–120 Hz.
- **Compression:** heavy-ish bus/tape glue, roughly 3–6 dB of gain reduction. The "saturated drums" (cited) suggest the whole mix is driven.
- **Modulation:** slow tape-style pitch wobble, about 0.4–0.8 Hz at ±5–12 cents ("warm and warped", cited). The funk material suits a slow phaser around 0.8–1 Hz.
- **Pitch:** standard tuning implied. No baritone and no documented octave effects.
- **Delay:** sparse. Short slap or ADT doubling (15–20 ms on doubles, about 160 ms slap) with low feedback, under 20 %.
- **Reverb:** small rooms (0.4–1.2 s). "Caroline" has a warm, empty space of about 2 s.
- **Gating/chops:** none documented.
- **Lo-fi:** described as lo-fi DIY (jenesaispop, cited), with voice-memo textures (cited). Expect phone-band artefacts, tape wobble and occasional dropouts.
- **Stereo:** guitars mostly mono-centred or as a single double. Width comes from slight random drift.
- **Playing:** clean arpeggios (cited, cops), funk chanks (KALX "glam funk"), and expressive lead outros ("Losing My Grip", "im not your man", both cited).

## 4. Reference songs
1. **"the cops are coming" (2026):** guitar-arpeggio bed, saturated drums and guitars that sound cassette-played, psychedelic instrumental outro. Self-mixed and self-mastered.
2. **"Losing My Grip" (2020):** soul groove with a ska feel, then a Beatlesque outro guitar solo. Mixed by Ricky Reed.
3. **"Creep" (2020):** mid-tempo psych-pop over a "warm and warped" backing.
4. **"Caroline" (2023):** close to acoustic folk, falsetto, strings, "empty and warm".
5. **"im not your man" (2025):** processed piano; guitar comes in from verse 2 and leads the outro.
6. ***Cirque Du Freak*** ("Risen Up", "Paranoia Dreams"): glam-funk soundscape, all instruments by Mesa.

order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.42, 0.50, 0.40, 0.24, 0.18, 0.20, 1.0, 0.55]

why: TEX CASSETTE 0.55 (worn & wobbly) = "old cassette in a car's rattling speakers" (iwantmymtvback).
