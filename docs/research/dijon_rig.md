## 2. Verified facts

**Recording approach**

*Absolutely*
- Recorded in Dijon's "very boxy" spare room in Highland Park, with an AKG C414 set to **omni** through a Heritage 1073, driven "as hot as possible" without clipping [R].
- "A lot of the music sounds so thin" because it is mostly room pickup. To tame the boxiness they "take out most of the mids with a graphic EQ" [R].
- Everything was recorded straight into Ableton through the interface's limitations. They avoided strummed chords: "a lot of jumpy picking" [R].
- Mixing used only a few plugins in Ableton on Jack Karaszewski's PC: "learn how to use compressors… for the first time", aiming for "varying fidelities" [R].
- "Big Mike's" opens with **feedback swells**. Dijon calls the album's noise bursts, slide stabs and feedback "gestural explosions" [R].
- Other gear on the album: a Make Noise Strega on "Talk Down", a Mutable Instruments Rings loop that is the basis of "Did You See It?", and an Octatrack loaded with breakbeats [R].

*Baby*
- Sessions were all-night jams around an Octatrack, an MPC and a TR-8 [MR].
- Vocals were sung on a 414 or an SM57 "with the mains as loud as they can go". The apparent "processing" is **bleed** of the whole song into the vocal mic [MR].
- Dijon finished the album alone in his garage studio over about two months [MR].
- The New Yorker, quoted in [WB]: "warm electric guitar licks dissolve into noisy FX, FM radio rips"; piano is "re-amped and fried".

**Who plays guitar on the records**
- *Baby* [WB]:
  - Dijon: guitar on tracks 1, 2 and 4–6, **fuzz guitar on "Yamaha"**, **resonator guitar on "Loyal & Marie"**.
  - Henry Kwapis: **fuzz guitar on "Another Baby!"**.
  - Ryan Richter: guitar on "Higher!" and "Automatic".
  - Mk.gee: production, drums, bass and loops, but no guitar credit.
- *Absolutely* [WA]: Dijon and Mk.gee on guitar; Noah Le Gros on guitar and **slide**.

**Dijon's own instruments**
- A white Fender Stratocaster, seen live in 2019 [EB, photo].
- A Jamstik Studio MIDI guitar, seen on the *Baby* tour in 2025 [EB, video].
- A Fender Bass VI [EB, Instagram].
- A "black electric guitar" on "(Referee)" live [AP].
- A **Fairfield Circuitry Shallow Water** on his pedalboard [EB, Instagram]. It is a short delay line (a bucket-brigade/BBD-style chip) whose time is modulated by a **pseudorandom, non-cyclic** signal, with a dynamics-driven **low-pass gate**. The result is a "non cyclical vibrato/chorus… old tape flavours" [SW].
- Rack and studio gear seen in photos or videos [EB]: Eventide H3000, Bastl Thyme, Bastl microgranny 2.0 (a lo-fi sampler), UA 4-710D, Distressor, KT-2A.
- On the Bastl Thyme in the "Big Mike's" live video, Dijon writes that he "ran some feedback samples into [it] and further mangled with the gnarly feedback/modulation robots" [EB].
- Background: as a teenager he made lo-fi Grouper-style songs on a **four-track recorder and a guitar** [PF].

**Live rig**
- 2022 tour, Mk.gee [FJ]:
  - Guitar: a "60s reissue Jaguar… modified… kind of baritone range".
  - Pedals: a Boss **BF-2 flanger**, a delay he "barely" uses, and a Chase Bliss Mood "for pitch and some reverb". The Rainger FX Reverb-X is his "secret weapon", used "for a crazy distorted kind of crunched-out reverb".
  - Everything runs into the old four-track used as a **mixer**, on one channel. It has headroom "but it kind of breaks up if you're playing really really hard". He goes direct; a Roland JC is used only for monitoring.
- Mk.gee today: baritone strings with flatwound strings, a Roland GK-2A pickup feeding a VG-8, and a Tascam 424 used as his preamp [WM, JHS].
- 2022 tour, Ryan Richter [FJ]: lap steel with Bill Asher pickups, into a Fender Princeton with a 12" speaker that never breaks up, plus a Boss RV-6 reverb and a compressor. His Strat has an Eric Johnson neck.
- 2022 tour, Jack Karaszewski [FJ]:
  - Uses a Eurorack skiff as a guitar pedalboard, with volume pedals that bring up "big spurts of noise".
  - Adds fuzz, overdrive and reverb pedals, and "reversing, pitching down… tape-like craziness".
  - He also "mangles our sound entirely while we're playing".
- 2022 tour, Dijon: "harsh noise sweeps" through a **VCA that only opens when he touches a sensor strip**, played between his vocal phrases [R].
- *Baby* tour, 2025–26:
  - Mike Haldeman's guitar is "compressed, overdriven… bursts of lightning" [AP], playing "crunchy riffs" written by Mk.gee [RSUK].
  - Haldeman's guitar is "whirling" on "Kindalove" and "swirling" on "The Dress" [AP].
  - Dijon and Jack work samples and an onstage mixing desk as instruments [RSUK, AP].
- Coachella 2026: **no amps on stage**. Richter used a computer amp sim (a Mixwave "Benson" plugin) and an EJ Strat on the neck pickup because he needed the "wide-open upper frequencies" to cut through the mix [FJ547].

## 3. Sonic signature in measurable terms
Items marked **[inference]** are my estimates; the rest follow the facts above.

- **Gain / saturation.** Clean to edge-of-breakup, set by picking force; this comes from the mixer/424 "breaks up only when hit hard". Fuzz is reserved for specific parts ("Yamaha", "Another Baby!"). Live, the riffs are compressed overdrive. **[inference]** Peak THD of about 3–10 %, rising with attack.
- **EQ.** Thin, with the mids scooped [R]. **[inference]** About −4 to −6 dB at 400–900 Hz, with a 2–4 kHz room "snap". Live guitar keeps its Strat-like upper top end [FJ547].
- **Compression.** Strong, roughly 4:1 or more **[inference]**. Comes from hot-driven room mics, first-time compressor use and the "compressed" live riffs.
- **Modulation.**
  - Shallow Water: random, non-cyclic pitch wobble. **[inference]** About ±8–15 cents at an effective 0.5–2 Hz, darkened by the low-pass gate.
  - BF-2 flanger: slow sweep, **[inference]** about 0.2–0.5 Hz.
  - "Whirling/swirling" lines live.
- **Pitch.**
  - Dijon plays in standard tuning ("Many Times": G major, about 130 BPM, Em–D–C–G [UG], community-sourced).
  - Mk.gee's parts sit in baritone range [FJ]. **[inference]** −3 to −5 semitones.
  - Slide and resonator colour on some songs.
- **Delay.** Bastl Thyme run into feedback (repeats that pile up and mangle), and the Mood's micro-looping. **[inference]** 150–300 ms at 60–90 % feedback during swells.
- **Reverb.**
  - Small boxy room, about 0.3–0.6 s **[inference]**.
  - Mk.gee's distorted, crushed small reverb (Reverb-X).
  - "Cavernous echo" on vocals live [AP].
- **Gating / chopping.**
  - Gestural: noise is opened by the VCA touch strip and by volume pedals [R, FJ].
  - Sample chops from the Octatrack, MPC and S2400.
- **Lo-fi.** Four-track roots, 8-bit grain from the microgranny, FM/radio "rips" [WB], bleed.
- **Width.** **[inference]** Mostly mono-ish room. Width comes from the bleed and the omni room, not from stereo effects.
- **Dynamics.** Very wide. Quiet jumpy picking erupts into noise bursts, so presets must respond strongly to playing level.

## 4. Reference songs
- **"Big Mike's"** (*Absolutely*): opens with feedback swells. Mk.gee's sparse, "manic rhythmic jumps" leave space for the vocal, and the guitar interplays with it. Live, Dijon feeds feedback samples into a Bastl Thyme [R, EB].
- **"Many Times"**: a thin roomy band take; Em–D–C–G at about 130 BPM [UG]. On the 2025 tour it opened with Haldeman's compressed, overdriven riff [AP].
- **"Rodeo Clown"**: Americana slide colour from Noah Le Gros [WA]. Wailing dynamics; a set-closer live [AP].
- **"Yamaha"** (*Baby*): Dijon's **fuzz guitar** in an '80s-arena context [WB, RSUK].
- **"Another Baby!"**: Kwapis's **fuzz guitar**. Live, "guitar tones roared" over swarming bass [WB, AP].
- **"Kindalove"**: a slow jam over a one-note synth drone, with whirling guitar [AP].

## 5. WARMFUL recipes

```
name: "DIJ Omni Room"
description: "Absolutely-era boxy spare room: thin, scooped, squeezed. Pick jumpy single notes, don't strum."
effects: [SWEETEN, DOUBLER, SPACE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, DIFF, TEXT, MOVE]
primary:   [0.62, 0.55, 0.12, 0.25, 0.06, 0.32, 1.0, 0.62]
secondary: [0.60, 0.50, 0.15, 0.40, 0.10, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why: SWEETEN dips 450 Hz and lifts 2.8k/8k, the closest thing to "took out most of the mids with a graphic EQ" [R]; TILT 0.62 thins it further.
why: SPACE at TIME 0.06 is a ~0.4 s chamber, standing in for the "very boxy" room picked up by an omni mic [R].
why: SQUASH after the room pumps the room up like a hot-driven 414 and first-time compressor use [R]; at 0.62 it stays below the overdrive point.
why: DOUBLER RATE 0.12 (~15 ms) at low level imitates the early reflection and bleed of one omni mic catching everything [R, MR].
```

```
name: "DIJ Big Mike's Swell"
description: "Feedback-swell intro: notes bloom in, repeats pile up and warp. Hold notes, ride the volume knob."
effects: [SWELL, VIBRATO, REELS, CASSETTE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.45, 0.62, 0.40, 0.22, 0.60, 0.80, 1.0, 0.38]
secondary: [0.62, 0.55, 0.60, 0.50, 0.55, 0.55, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why: SWELL at AMOUNT 0.62 gives about a 0.55 s attack, recreating the feedback swells that open "Big Mike's" [R].
why: VIBRATO at RATE 0.4 (~0.7 Hz), ±7 cents, with drift 0.6 adds the random wobble of a Shallow Water [EB, SW].
why: REELS at 0.6 (~245 ms) with feedback around 0.75 and DRIFT 0.55 makes "feedback samples… mangled with the gnarly feedback/modulation" of the Bastl Thyme [EB].
why: CASSETTE at 0.38 gives worn four-track grain, after his teenage four-track songs [PF].
```

```
name: "DIJ Shallow Water"
description: "Dijon's board chorus: random, non-repeating pitch drift, dulled top end. Slow-jam chords and Kindalove lines."
effects: [SWEETEN, VIBRATO, SPACE, FILTER]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.45, 0.30, 0.50, 0.34, 0.22, 0.22, 0.72, 0.58]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.85, 0.50, 0.30, 0.50, 0.55, 0.50]
filterStyle: LowPass
why: VIBRATO with DRIFT 0.85 is random rather than a sine, at about 1.1 Hz and ±12 cents. MIX 0.72 blends in dry signal so it reads as chorus. This matches Shallow Water's pseudorandom, non-cyclic delay modulation [EB, SW].
why: FILTER in LowPass at 0.58 stands in for the pedal's low-pass gate damping [SW]. WARMFUL can't make the filter respond to dynamics, so it is fixed.
why: SWEETEN at 0.3 adds gentle compression and warmth, giving the "whirling/swirling" sustain heard live [AP].
why: SPACE at 0.22 is a small room/plate kept low, staying with the dry, roomy aesthetic [R].
```

```
name: "DIJ Lightning Riff"
description: "Baby-tour crunch: compressed overdrive into crushed small reverb with slow flange. Dig in for bursts."
effects: [DRIVE, PHASER, SPACE, SQUASH]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [DIFF, CHAR, TEXT, MOVE]
primary:   [0.60, 0.52, 0.30, 0.72, 0.12, 0.30, 1.0, 0.70]
secondary: [0.58, 0.45, 0.20, 0.50, 0.15, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why: DRIVE at 0.52 (about +19 dB) is touch-dependent breakup, like Mk.gee's mixer that "breaks up if you're playing really hard" [FJ]. Live it is the "compressed, overdriven… lightning" riff [AP].
why: SPACE placed before DRIVE gives the "distorted, crunched-out reverb" of the Rainger Reverb-X [FJ]; TIME 0.12 keeps it small.
why: SQUASH at 0.70 provides the "compressed" part of Haldeman's tone [AP].
why: PHASER at AMOUNT 0.72 (many stages, flange-like) at RATE 0.3 (~0.42 Hz) stands in for the Boss BF-2 flanger [FJ].
```

```
name: "DIJ Yamaha Fuzz"
description: "Baby-era fuzz guitar torn by radio static and sampler stutter. Short phrases, let the loop chew them."
effects: [FUZZ, TREMOLO, COLLAGE, INTERFERENCE]
moduleOn: [true, false, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.35, 0.45, 0.50, 0.00, 0.48, 0.40, 1.0, 0.26]
secondary: [0.55, 0.42, 0.00, 0.50, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why: FUZZ at TILT 0.35 is round with little gating, matching Dijon's "fuzz guitar" on "Yamaha" and Kwapis's on "Another Baby!" [WB].
why: COLLAGE at 0.48 (~225 ms) with DRIFT 0.4 gives random double-speed stutters, standing in for the Octatrack/S2400 chop-collage method [MR, EB].
why: INTERFERENCE at 0.26 gives AM-radio band-limiting and crackle, the "noisy FX, FM radio rips" [WB].
why: MOVE is OFF; the "gestural" VCA noise bursts [R] are left to the player's volume knob.
```

## 6. Confidence and open questions
- **High:** the *Absolutely* recording method, the 2022 tour rigs, the *Baby* guitar credits and the no-amp Coachella setup. All come from first-person sources or official credits.
- **Medium:** the Shallow Water, Strat and Jamstik sightings, which rest on Equipboard photos only.
- **Low:** every numeric Hz, cents and dB value in section 3 is my inference. There are no published settings.
- **Not found:**
  - Dijon's actual studio guitar model and amp on *Baby*.
  - His string gauges.
  - The make of his "black electric guitar".
  - Whether the Shallow Water sits on guitar or on vocals/samplers.
- **Still unconfirmed:**
  - The guitar on the SNL "Higher!" performance (YouTube DN-73CtVm8o).
  - Haldeman's rig (Richter calls it "technology"-heavy).
- **No duplicates:** the DIJ recipes avoid the existing MKG ones; "MKG Distorted Room" is close to "DIJ Lightning Riff". Check the two side by side before shipping both.
