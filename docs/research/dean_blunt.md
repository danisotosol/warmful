## 2. Verified facts
- **Recording method:** Blunt records to tape. He tapes over a tape as soon as it is transferred, so hiss and "ghosts" of earlier recordings bleed through. One channel can end before the other. Everything is played live with no sequencing, and timing and tuning are left uncorrected. He refused a mastering pass that "sounded robotic" ([artsdesk](https://theartsdesk.com/new-music/theartsdesk-qa-electronic-musicians-hype-williams)).
- **Early Hype Williams:** the duo began with "murky guitar psychedelia" ([Quietus 2011](https://thequietus.com/opinion-and-essays/black-sky-thinking/hype-williams-interview/)). Samples are "motheaten by echo" ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2012/apr/05/hype-williams-speak-the-truth)).
- **Gear (crowd-sourced, low authority):** SP-303 sampler, SH-101, Alpha Juno-1, a Line 6 POD X3 seen in a photo, a white Strat, and a Vintage V300 acoustic ([Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/pros/dean-blunt)). No amp or pedal is documented anywhere.
- **Who plays guitar:** Joanne Robertson plays most of the guitar on Black Metal. She uses improvised, unrepeatable tunings ([TCI](https://thecreativeindependent.com/people/painter-and-musician-joanne-robertson-on-why-its-never-just-you-creating-alone/)). Blunt adds guitar over the Pastels loop on "100" ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_(Dean_Blunt_song))). Credits on BM2 are disputed: secondary sources name Alex Blake (guitar) and Mica Levi.
- **Sampled jangle:** the jangle on Black Metal is often sampled. LUSH uses Big Star and "100" uses the Pastels ([Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/release/6240420-Dean-Blunt-Black-Metal)). Sample and live playing blur together ([RA](https://ra.co/reviews/15919)).
- **Black Metal processing:** reverb-heavy guitar and a looped feel ([musicOMH](https://www.musicomh.com/reviews/albums/dean-blunt-black-metal)). The strings are compressed and phone-like ([Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/dean-blunt-black-metal-2/)).
- **Black Metal 2 processing:** granular guitar loops ([RA](https://ra.co/reviews/34007)). A phaser or pitch shifter is switched in and out across the whole mix ([BPM](https://beatsperminute.com/album-review-dean-blunt-black-metal-2/)).
- **Babyfather:** pirate-radio tape distortion, sirens and noise bursts ([RA](https://ra.co/reviews/18815)).

## 3. Sonic signature (measurable; **[inference]** unless cited)
- **Gain:** clean to edge-of-breakup. The colour comes from tape and console saturation, not distortion. Exceptions are the noise blasts on COUNTRY, PROLIFIC DAEMONS and Flames.
- **EQ:** mid-forward and band-limited, roughly 150 Hz–7 kHz, from tape and phone-quality sources. High end rolls off at about 6–8 kHz. Robertson's low tunings add body around 100–200 Hz.
- **Compression:** heavy and squashed on samples and strings, "compressed, muted" ([Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/dean-blunt-black-metal-2/)). Guitars themselves are lightly compressed.
- **Modulation:** tape wow at about 0.4–1 Hz, ±5–12 cents. The "washed-out stereo" backing guitar ([KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=572299)) suggests ADT or chorus at about 15–25 ms. The phaser on ZaZa ([BPM](https://beatsperminute.com/album-review-dean-blunt-black-metal-2/)) sounds like a slow sweep, about 0.5–1 Hz, switched in and out.
- **Pitch:** guitars are slightly out of tune, up to about ±20 cents ("untuned", [Quietus](https://thequietus.com/quietus-reviews/dean-blunt-black-metal-review/), [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/dean-blunt-soul-on-fire/)). Robertson tunes well below concert pitch.
- **Delay:** dub-style echo on samples, about 200–400 ms with 30–50 % feedback, dark.
- **Reverb:** room to hall, about 1.5–2.5 s, fairly wet (30–45 %). It is lighter on The Redeemer, which "did away with much of the reverb" ([Quietus](https://thequietus.com/quietus-reviews/dean-blunt-black-metal-review/)).
- **Gating / chopping:** comes from abrupt edits, hard cuts, loops cut mid-phrase and fades ([Black Is Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Is_Beautiful_(Dean_Blunt_and_Inga_Copeland_album))), not from a gate pedal.
- **Lo-fi artefacts:** hiss, print-through ghosts, dropouts, sample-rate grit from the SP-303 era, and YouTube-rip codec damage.
- **Stereo:** mostly narrow and mono-ish; wide only on the doubled backing guitars.
- **Dynamics:** soft, tentative strums and fingerpicking with loose timing.

## 4. Reference songs
1. **LUSH (Black Metal):** Big Star string sample with reverb-heavy jangly guitar, looping drums.
2. **50 CENT (BM):** capo'd Am7–Em loop played 16 times, reverb-drenched ([AllMusic via search](https://www.allmusic.com/album/black-metal-mw0002745864)), washed stereo backing guitar.
3. **100 (BM):** Pastels loop with extra guitar on top, bright and dry-ish, "road music".
4. **SEMTEX (BM2):** granular, hallucinogenic guitar loops; "here we are, back on the guitar".
5. **ZaZa (BM2):** twangy riff, then a phaser or pitch shifter across the whole mix, with skips and warps.
6. **NBA (Soul on Fire):** tentative, untuned strums over a primitive beat, with the casual feel of a voice memo.

## 5. WARMFUL recipes

```
name: "BLT Black Metal Jangle"
description: "Reverb-washed, doubled clean jangle in the style of LUSH/50 CENT. Strum soft open chords or loop a two-chord figure."
effects: [SWEETEN, DOUBLER, SPACE, CASSETTE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.55, 0.30, 0.23, 0.45, 0.42, 0.40, 1.0, 0.20]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.20, 0.50, 0.25, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- SWEETEN: gentle compression and an upper-mid lift give the squashed-but-clean sample-jangle feel (Pitchfork "compressed").
- DOUBLER 20 ms, ~20 c drift: the "washed-out stereo" backing guitar on 50 CENT (KVR).
- SPACE ~2 s hall at 40 % wet: "reverb-heavy… Britpop-like guitar" (musicOMH).
- CASSETTE 0.2: clean deck warmth and a slight HF roll-off; Blunt records to tape (artsdesk).
```

name: "BLT Tape Ghosts"
description: "Worn, overdubbed 4-track with wow and dark echo. Hype Williams-era murky guitar psychedelia; play sparse."
effects: [DRIVE, VIBRATO, REELS, CASSETTE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.40, 0.20, 0.34, 0.25, 0.60, 0.30, 1.0, 0.55]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.60, 0.50, 0.50, 0.45, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- DRIVE 0.2, dark tilt: tape and console warmth, no pedal distortion (no gear documented).
- VIBRATO 0.5 Hz, ±9 c, high drift: wow from reused tapes that "just get worse" (artsdesk).
- REELS ~245 ms, low feedback, more drift: samples "motheaten by echo" (Guardian).
- CASSETTE 0.55: worn tape with hiss and dropouts, standing in for hiss and ghost overdubs (artsdesk).
```

name: "BLT Semtex Loop"
description: "Granular, crinkly guitar micro-loops in the style of SEMTEX. Play a phrase and let it stack and warp; move TIME for edits."
effects: [SWEETEN, PITCH, COLLAGE, CASSETTE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.50, 0.25, 0.50, 0.40, 0.75, 0.75, 1.0, 0.35]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.40, 0.50, 0.35, 0.55, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- SWEETEN: light compression so the loops sit flat and dense.
- PITCH 0 st at 40 % mix with lo-fi drift 0.4: granular sample-rate grit, the "granular, hallucinogenic guitar loops" (RA) and SP-303 sampling.
- COLLAGE ~765 ms loop, feedback 0.75, drift: "scrappy loop-based" songs (Turntable Lab), loops cut and repeated off the grid.
- CASSETTE 0.35: the "crinkly lo-fi setting" (RA).
```

```
name: "BLT ZaZa Phase"
description: "Twangy dry riff with a slow phaser across the full wet signal; toggle the module for ZaZa-style warps."
effects: [DRIVE, PHASER, SPACE, CASSETTE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, DIFF, MOVE, TEXT]
primary:   [0.62, 0.15, 0.44, 0.60, 0.25, 0.25, 1.0, 0.15]
secondary: [0.50, 0.50, 0.30, 0.50, 0.20, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- DRIVE 0.15, bright tilt: the twangy riff (BPM).
- SPACE before PHASER, room 1.2 s: puts the reverb inside the sweep, the way the whole mix is phased (BPM "entire mix").
- PHASER 0.8 Hz, deep, random drift: "subtle lysergic skips and warps" (Quietus).
- CASSETTE 0.15: a warm deck to glue it.
```

```
name: "BLT Pirate Chop"
description: "Babyfather pirate-radio guitar: hard square chop, dub echo, radio-band crunch. Stab chords and let it cut."
effects: [DRIVE, TREMOLO, CASCADE, INTERFERENCE]
moduleOn: [true, true, true, true]
order: [CHAR, MOVE, DIFF, TEXT]
primary:   [0.45, 0.35, 0.70, 0.80, 0.66, 0.55, 1.0, 0.30]
secondary: [0.55, 0.50, 0.25, 0.50, 0.40, 0.50, 0.50, 0.50]
filterStyle: Tilt
why:
- DRIVE 0.35: "grotty distortion, as if recorded to tape" (RA BBF).
- TREMOLO ~2.9 Hz square, depth 0.8, some drift: stands in for the abrupt edits and hard cuts (Black Is Beautiful) and the gated chop; WARMFUL has no gate.
- CASCADE 250 ms, ~50 % feedback, dark BBD: the "digi-dub loops" (RA BBF) and dub echo.
- INTERFERENCE 0.3: AM-radio band-limiting and static, the pirate-radio framing (RA BBF).
```

## 6. Confidence and open questions
- **High:** the tape-based, live, uncorrected workflow; heavy reverb on Black Metal; sampled jangle; Robertson's loose, untuned guitar; loops and phasing on BM2.
- **Medium:** whether the BM2 phasing is a phaser or a pitch shifter; who played guitar on BM2 (Robertson, Alex Blake or Levi).
- **Low:** any guitar, amp or pedal chain. None is documented, and the Equipboard entries are crowd-sourced; the POD X3 is the only guitar-processing item, seen in a photo.
- **Open:** Blunt has given no making-of interview with gear details. The Wire interview (David Keenan, 2014) may contain more but is paywalled.
- All ms, Hz and cent values in §3 are estimates by ear and description **[inference]**.
